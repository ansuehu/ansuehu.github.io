Latxa-Omni

Spoken conversation in Basque: speech in, text and speech out.

Code

How it works

A Whisper encoder turns the user's speech into embeddings, which a projector feeds to a Latxa language model (the thinker). The thinker writes the answer as text, and an autoregressive talker turns that text into SNAC audio codes, decoded to 24 kHz speech.

Speech→ Whisper encoder→ Latxa thinker→ SNAC talker→ Speech
